About JNNCE

College Overview

Jawaharlal Nehru New College of Engineering (JNNCE) is one of the premier engineering institutions providing quality technical education, advanced laboratories, modern infrastructure, placements and industry-oriented learning environment for students.

Campus Facilities

Library

Spacious digital library with thousands of technical books, journals, research papers and e-learning resources.

Hostel Facility

Safe and comfortable hostel accommodation with Wi-Fi, hygienic food and 24/7 security facilities.

Green Campus

Beautiful eco-friendly campus with smart classrooms, laboratories and modern infrastructure.

Bus Facility

College transportation facility covering multiple routes for safe and convenient student travel.

Sports Complex

Excellent sports facilities for cricket, football, volleyball, badminton and indoor games.

Auditorium

Modern auditorium equipped for seminars, workshops, technical events and cultural programs.
